T and I did a snacky grocery store stroll Friday night. Best time to do groceries—barely anyone in the aisles!

The goal, as you might surmise, was to restock our snack supplies. But I couldn’t pass up the $7 half-bushel box of sweet peppers. 

If you, unlike me, have ever before bought a half-bushel box of sweet peppers, you know where I went wrong. This is so many peppers!! I sliced and diced for an hour and there’s still one layer in the box. Some, mercifully, I put in the fridge for the week; the rest, optimistically, I’ll finish chopping and freeze tomorrow.

Anyway, if you’ve ideas for pepper recipes, do let me know.
