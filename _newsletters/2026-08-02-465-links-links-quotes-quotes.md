Hello!

Writing this in between visits with friends and other things going on this weekend, so will move straight to the links:

- Deb Chachra’s latest _Metafoundry_, “[determinate tomatoes](https://buttondown.com/metafoundry/archive/determinate-tomatoes/)”, is full of lovely reflections and links. I’m eager to see what the rest of this generative year brings of Chachra’s writings.
- Robin Sloan delivered some thoughtful advice to his younger self in “[Fogbound](https://www.robinsloan.com/newsletters/fogbound/)”, the January edition of his newsletter. (And, as is the theme, links!!)
- [Mike Monteiro recently wrote a piece](https://buttondown.com/monteiro/archive/how-to-help-men-pull-their-heads-out-of-their/) which included this, quoting Marisa Kabas who was quoting Jamelle Bouie (quote me, we can be four or five levels deep on this!): “There is a cohort of men who resent the responsibilities and obligations of adulthood—of the fact that you owe things to other people.” WHEW!! Great piece.
- Mandy Brown on how [reading ought to be *more* than the practice of empathy](https://aworkinglibrary.com/writing/reading-matters-deeply): “To reduce reading to the practice of empathy is to divorce it of its real power and pleasure: to enjoy the fruits of another person’s creative effort, to think *with* the writer and their characters, not becoming them, *but becoming more fully yourself*.”
- Bruce Schneier offers [a framework for deciding when to use AI](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/07/should-you-use-ai-for-a-task-heres-a-simple-way-to-decide.html): should I approach this task as work (AI may be fine), or exercise (AI atrophies the very skill you’re trying to develop)? 
- Excellent to see that [online passport renewals will now be generally available in Canada](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/online-passport-renewal-canada-9.7287458). Huge props to the teams behind this!
- Dave Rupert offers [a tangible example of “voting with your dollars”](https://daverupert.com/2026/07/number-chart-go-up/) by explaining how powerful the “number chart” can be: “Collectively we can influence the number chart. It could be a coordinated buying campaign or boycott, but I think it could be as simple as re-patronizing small businesses you already enjoy.”
- [Debbie Millman’s responses to the Oldster Magazine questionnaire](https://oldster.substack.com/p/this-is-64-design-maven-and-more) are delightful.

All the best for the week ahead!
